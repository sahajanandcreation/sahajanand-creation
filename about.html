About Us

An Introduction to The Sahajanand Creation

Handmade with care, in the heart of Surat

The Sahajanand Creation

We make and sell ladies' lace, neckpieces, phone covers and a range of small and large fancy items. Every product is handmade and crafted with good quality.

Our shop is located in Nana Varachha, Surat, where you can visit in person to see our products, or order directly on WhatsApp.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you take custom orders?
Yes, we make custom lace, neckpieces and phone covers as per your design, color and size requirements. Message us on WhatsApp with your idea.
What are your shop timings?
We are open daily from 10 AM to 8 PM at Nana Varachha, Surat.
Do you deliver outside Surat?
Yes, we can ship products outside Surat. Please share your delivery address on WhatsApp and we'll confirm the shipping details.
How can I place an order?
You can add products to your cart on this website and send the order via WhatsApp, or message us directly on WhatsApp with what you'd like.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept cash and UPI payments. Payment details will be shared once your order is confirmed on WhatsApp.
Can I exchange or return a product?
Since most items are handmade or made to order, please check details carefully before ordering. For any issue with your order, contact us on WhatsApp and we'll do our best to help.
